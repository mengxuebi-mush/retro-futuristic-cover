## Scope and controls

Four cases used the same `EDITORIAL_POSTER` carrier, `ASYMMETRIC_COUNTERWEIGHT` layout, `SINGLE_OBJECT` assembly, `SPECULATIVE_OBJECT` origin, `FUTURE_HABITAT_CAPSULE` family, `P01_POWDER_SIGNAL` palette, `DUAL_FIELD` gradient field, `CENTER_OF_GRAVITY` gradient topology, `PALETTE_ASSIMILATED` treatment, `INTEGRATED_HERO` scale role, `SINGLE_SCALE_ANOMALY` focal event, `CJK_NEO_GROTESK` type mode, and exact seven-string poster copy. The only intended changes were hypothetical function, `CUSTOM_COMPOSED` morphology, and its 2–4 purpose-bearing parts.

The four custom specifications were authored before generation and validated against `design-system/custom-morphology.schema.json`. Raw and rejected generations remain outside the repository. The selected proofs and selector manifests are the compact evidence set; `summary.json` is the aggregate record and `verdicts/*.verdict.json` contains one independently valid visual verdict per case.

## Interpretations

- All four selected proofs pass machine delivery, full-size inspection, thumbnail inspection, title-first hierarchy, support attachment, subject-mass unity, function recognition, and continuous opaque-background checks.
- All four silhouettes remain distinct and retain their declared shape ancestry. The toroidal loop, hinged arch, accordion fan, and braided figure-eight are meaningfully different from one another.
- At least two cases are clearly outside the four optional archetype families: the toroidal ribbon commons and braided hammock pair. The hinged arch dialogue and accordion threshold review are adjacent to soft vertical infrastructure and biomorphic apparatus because of their rounded vaulted construction, but neither fully collapses into those archetypes.
- The morphology definitions successfully carry function into structure: suspension and cavity for prototyping, threshold and facing chamber for exchange, repeated bays and shared table for review, and hammock plus braided ribs for paired building.
- The selected objects preserve rounded, buoyant, warm retro-futurist character through fiberglass/enamel/lacquer, resin or bubble glass, warm metal, and upholstery. None visibly collapses into a current EV, appliance, phone, or cold minimal architecture.

## Repeated failures and generator variance

- The repeated hard failure was title hierarchy in the initial generations of 2/4 cases: `BUILD` rendered larger than `VIBE CODING WORKSHOP`. Each received exactly one allowed hierarchy-only retry; both selected proofs pass.
- The quiet-pair initial generation had a separate palette failure: it visibly drifted to blush/apricot instead of locked powder-sky + warm-ivory. Its unused single retry was spent on a palette-only regeneration; the selected retry passes while preserving morphology, copy, composition, and other locked variables.
- No custom-morphology failure repeated across cases. There is no repeated archetype-collapse pattern.
- One selected proof contains small unplanned pictogram-like support marks. This is minor generator variance, not extra readable copy or a hard verdict failure.
- One selected proof contains a faint contextual horizon. It is a watch item against literal-scene drift, but it does not obscure function, silhouette, title hierarchy, or the required opaque gradient.
- Proofs were normalized from the generator's 1086 × 1448 output to 1080 × 1440 without changing the 3:4 composition. The final machine checks are exact.

## Handoff conclusion

`CUSTOM_COMPOSED` passed this four-case morphology stress test without a full archetype collapse. At least two silhouettes are clearly outside the old archetype set; hinged arch and accordion threshold sit near soft infrastructure/biomorphic territory but remain distinct. The only repeated failure class was generator typography hierarchy, appearing before retry in two cases; the quiet-pair palette drift was a single-case generator failure corrected by its one retry. Neither finding justifies a grammar change. No exact grammar wording change is proposed from this evidence task.
